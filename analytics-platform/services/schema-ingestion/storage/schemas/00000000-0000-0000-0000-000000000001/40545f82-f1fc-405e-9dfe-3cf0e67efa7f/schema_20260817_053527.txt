category_id: A unique numerical identifier for each category, which cannot be empty. This is the primary key for the table.
category_name: The name of the category, which cannot be empty.
parent_category_id: A numerical identifier that links a category to its parent category; this column can be empty if the category has no parent.

The inventory_movements table tracks changes in product quantities within a stock system. 

Here's a breakdown of the table's columns:

movement_id: A unique, automatically generated integer that serves as the primary identifier for each inventory movement record. This field cannot be left blank.

product_id: A large integer that identifies the specific product involved in the movement. This field cannot be left blank.

movement_date: A timestamp that records the date and time when the inventory movement occurred. This field cannot be left blank.

movement_type: A text string (up to 20 characters) that describes the type of movement, such as "sale," "purchase," or "adjustment." This field cannot be left blank.

quantity: An integer representing the number of units of the product that were moved. This field cannot be left blank.

reference_id: A large integer that may be used to link this movement to another related record, such as an order or invoice.

The `orders` table stores information about customer orders. Each order has a unique identifier, a link to the customer who placed it, and the date it was placed. It also includes details about the shipping address and the cost of shipping, as well as any associated coupon information.

Here's a breakdown of the table's columns:

*   `order_id`: A unique, large integer that serves as the primary identifier for each order and cannot be left blank.

*   `order_status`: A text string of up to 30 characters that describes the current status of the order and cannot be left blank.
*   `shipping_city`: A text string of up to 80 characters that specifies the destination city for the order and cannot be left blank.
*   `shipping_state`: A text string of up to 50 characters that specifies the destination state for the order and cannot be left blank.
*   `shipping_fee`: A numeric value that represents the cost of shipping, with a total of 10 digits and 2 decimal places, and cannot be left blank.
*   `coupon_code`: An optional text string of up to 30 characters that represents a coupon code used for the order.
*   `coupon_discount`: A numeric value that represents the discount amount applied by the coupon, with a total of 10 digits and 2 decimal places, and cannot be left blank.
